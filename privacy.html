Jarful Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

Jarful is an app by Shahil Kanjee. Questions about this policy or your data: support@i5life.com.

The short version

Jarful keeps your journal in local app storage on your iPhone. It has no accounts, developer analytics, advertising or tracking, and no feature that uploads your journal to us.

This does not mean every copy always stays on the phone: you choose where to save exported backups, and iOS backups follow your Apple settings. Visiting this website and emailing support are separate from using the app.

What Jarful stores

Jarful stores the moments you write or dictate and their dates, optional people and theme tags, favourites, saved reflections, their original suggested wording and source links, and unfinished drafts. Any existing stored attachments are also preserved in its local storage and backups.

There is no developer-access or journal-upload service in the app. If you choose to email private content or share an exported file outside Jarful, that is a separate action under your control.

Voice capture

Voice capture starts only when you tap Start recording and allow microphone and speech access. Jarful uses iOS speech recognition and requires on-device recognition; if that is unavailable or permission is denied, you can type instead. There is no cloud-recognition fallback in Jarful.

Jarful uses microphone buffers during recognition but does not save an audio recording. Your transcript can be kept as a local draft; you can review and edit it before saving a moment.

AI features

Jarful’s optional reflections are created with Apple’s on-device Foundation Model, only when you request one. Your selected moments are not sent to a remote AI service by this feature. Jarful has no ChatGPT sign-in or remote AI fallback.

You can review and edit the proposed reflection before keeping it. Availability depends on your device and settings. Typed entries, the Jar, search, calendar, and backup and restore do not need an AI model.

Permissions

Microphone permission: “Record a good moment when you tap Start recording. Audio is transcribed on this device.”

Speech recognition permission: “Turn your spoken moments into text on this device so you can review and edit them before saving.”

You can use Jarful by typing without granting either permission. Version 1 does not request notification permission or schedule reminders.

Backups and exports

You can create one backup file and choose where to save it in Files. It contains saved moments, dates, tags, favourites, reflections and source links, plus capture drafts and any existing stored attachments. App settings and sign-in credentials are not included.

Encryption is on by default and uses a passphrase you choose, not a key tied to one device. The file uses AES-256-GCM encryption with a passphrase-derived key. Keep the passphrase separately: there is no developer recovery key, so we cannot recover a lost passphrase or unlock the file for you without it.

A plain, unencrypted backup is optional and requires acknowledging a warning. Anyone with that plain file can read it. Restore validates a file and asks for your review and confirmation before replacing saved records; it does not merge collections.

The Files provider you choose may transfer or store the backup in the cloud. Your iPhone’s own backups, including iCloud backups if enabled, are managed by Apple under your settings. Deleting local entries does not remove those separate copies.

People you write about

You may mention family or friends in a moment or add an optional person tag. That writing is stored with your local journal, under your control. If you export it, the backup follows the choices described above.

Keeping and deleting your data

Saved data remains until it is deleted, the app’s data is removed, or a reviewed restore replaces it. You can open a saved moment and choose Delete moment in the app. Its separate unfinished edit may remain as a draft.

You can discard an unfinished capture using Discard draft. Saved reflections can be edited, but there is currently no individual reflection-delete control. Use Privacy & data → Delete all data to review and remove the full local collection, including drafts, saved reflections and stored attachments. Deleting a source moment can leave a reflection with an unavailable source link. It does not remove wording already saved in that reflection or its original suggestion.

Deleting the app normally removes its local app data; offloading the app can keep it. Backup files you exported are not deleted automatically. Remove them separately in Files and manage other backups with Apple or your provider. Jarful does not promise secure erasure of historical database or backup copies.

Children

Jarful does not ask for a user’s age or date of birth and has no account or journal-upload service. Its local data handling does not depend on the user’s age.

Your rights

We do not receive your journal through the app, so we cannot retrieve or delete local entries on your behalf. You can view and edit saved moments, export your collection, delete individual moments and discard unfinished captures. Individual reflection deletion and full-collection deletion are explained above.

If you are in the EU or UK and have a question about your data rights, contact support@i5life.com. Emailing support shares the information you include in that message; do not include private entries or backup passphrases unless you intend to share them.
