Jarful Support

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Jarful is a place to collect and revisit good moments in your own words. Today has three open spaces, with no suggested topics. Leave any space empty.

Contact

Email support@i5life.com. We aim to reply within 3 working days.

Add a moment

Tap a blank numbered space on Today, type your words and tap Save. Optional Person and Theme tags can be left empty.

Use your voice

  1. Tap Capture a moment, then Start recording.
  2. Allow microphone and speech access. Recognition must be available on your device; otherwise use Type instead.
  3. Tap Stop and review. Check and edit the transcript.
  4. Tap Review this text, check the moment and tap Save.

Typing works without microphone or speech permission. You can change permission settings in iPhone Settings.

Edit, delete and find

Open a moment from Today or the Jar, edit its words, date or tags, then tap Save edits. To remove it, tap Delete moment and confirm. A separate unfinished edit may remain as a draft.

On Today, use Find a saved moment to search words, people, themes and saved reflections. Calendar lets you choose a past day, read entries and add a missed moment.

Looking Back groups moments by week or month. On supported devices you can request an optional on-device reflection, review and edit it before Keep reflection, and open its source moments. Core journaling works without an AI model.

Back up

  1. On Today, open the icon labelled Backup and restore.
  2. Leave Encrypt backup on. Choose a passphrase of at least 8 characters and repeat it.
  3. Tap Create backup file and choose a location in Files. The filename includes a date and time.
  4. Keep the file somewhere you can reach if the phone is lost. Keep its passphrase separately; a lost passphrase cannot be recovered.

Plain export is optional and requires acknowledging that anyone with the file can read it. Your selected Files provider may store backups in the cloud.

Restore

Restore replaces saved records; it does not merge them. Back up the current collection first. Use disposable example entries for your first restore test.

  1. Open Backup and restore, then Choose backup file.
  2. For an encrypted file, enter its passphrase and tap Unlock and review. For a plain file, tap Validate and review.
  3. Check the app, file date, record counts and any missing-source warning. Cancel if it is the wrong collection.
  4. Only when ready, acknowledge the review and tap Replace saved records.
  5. Reopen your moments, dates and reflections to check the collection.

Invalid files and incorrect passphrases produce an error before replacement. A valid backup may still be the wrong collection, so always read the review. Already-missing sources cannot be recovered from a file that did not contain them. App settings are not restored; save other unfinished work first.

Delete all your data

Open Privacy & data from Today, then Delete all data. Review the counts, make a backup first if you want to keep a copy, and confirm Delete all data. This removes local moments, drafts, saved reflections and stored attachments, including hidden preview photos. Cancel leaves them in place. Deleting the app using Delete App also normally removes its local container; Offload App can keep it.

Exported backups are separate: delete them in Files and manage any copies in iCloud or other provider/device backups separately. Deleting the app does not automatically delete these copies.

For individual records, open a moment and choose Delete moment. An unfinished capture can be removed with Discard draft. Saved reflections can be edited but have no individual delete control; they remain until Delete all data, app deletion or a reviewed restore removes or replaces them. Deleting a source moment does not remove wording already saved in a reflection or its original suggestion. No secure erasure of historical database or backup bytes is promised.

If something is wrong

Email support@i5life.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, Jarful version, what you tried, what happened and any error shown. No private entries are needed. Do not send backup passphrases.

Keep the app and its data intact while asking for help. Do not uninstall as your first troubleshooting step, because that can remove local entries.
